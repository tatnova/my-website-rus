<h2>About the project</h2>
<p>The studio offers machine-based body contouring: vacuum-roller anti-cellulite massage, cavitation and RF lifting. Its clients are mostly women. I joined the project in April 2026, when ads were already running in the account, so this case study covers only my period: 21 April to 20 October.</p>
<h3>What we had to keep in mind</h3>
<ul>
<li>A small ad budget, so every format had to justify its spend with inquiries.</li>
<li>Machine treatments require trust. Before visiting, a woman wants to know what the treatment is, what result to expect and what the first session costs.</li>
<li>All clients are from one city, so ads wear out quickly if the same thing runs for too long.</li>
</ul>
<div class="q"><img class="av" src="/uploads/cases/tatyana-richmann-avatar.jpg" alt="" aria-hidden="true"><div><blockquote>With a small budget, every shekel has to work. So I didn't stick to one format: I tested conversations and instant forms and kept whichever brought cheaper inquiries at the time.</blockquote><cite><b>Tatyana Richmann</b>, Meta Ads specialist</cite></div></div>
<h2>Campaign setup</h2>
<p>We worked in three directions:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>WhatsApp conversations.</b> The person taps the button and writes to the studio straight away.</li>
<li><b>Leads through a short instant form</b> right inside Instagram and Facebook. From June this became the main format.</li>
<li><b>Ads driving profile visits.</b> Low-cost warm-up: a woman sees the treatments, results and specialist, and later writes on her own.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Audience.</b> Women living in the city and nearby areas.</p>
<p><b>Placements.</b> Feed, Stories and Reels on Instagram and Facebook.</p>

<h2>Offers and ad creatives</h2>
<p>Every creative gave a clear reason to write: a specific treatment and the price of the first visit.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>A static RF lifting ad</b> with the price of a first trial session. The cheapest conversations of the period: 14 at ₪10.48, and it was clicked more often than any other creative (CTR 5.62%).</li>
<li><b>A vacuum-roller massage video</b> with the trial session price. The most conversations: 39 at ₪16.58.</li>
<li><b>A video from the specialist</b> about the belly and thighs staying a problem area even after weight loss. The main lead creative: 26 leads at ₪28.74, and its best run brought 9 leads at ₪19.52.</li>
<li><b>A static LPG massage ad</b> for profile visits: 1,216 visits at ₪0.19.</li>
</ul>
<p class="muted" style="font-size:15px">We don't show the original creatives: the client hasn't agreed to publish them.</p>
<h2>Launch and results</h2>
<p>In spring, conversations did most of the work. In June we launched instant forms and from then on switched between formats based on results: in August we briefly brought back conversations with new videos, and from September it was leads again. Ad sets were regularly refreshed with new creatives.</p>

<h3>What else the data showed</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Women aged 45–54 left the most leads</b>: 32 out of 85.</li>
<li><b>The cheapest leads came from Instagram Reels</b>, at ₪19.93. In Facebook Reels a lead cost twice as much, ₪42.77.</li>
</ul>

<ul>
<li>In six months: <b>101 conversations</b> at ₪16.01 and <b>85 instant form leads</b> at ₪29.80.</li>
