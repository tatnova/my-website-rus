<h2>About the project</h2>
<p>A private osteopath in Israel. The ads were in Russian and spoken by the osteopath herself: about the effects of tailbone injuries, children's posture, and how the abdomen affects overall wellbeing. They were shown to women aged 25+ in her city and nearby areas. The work started on 14 August 2026 and is ongoing.</p>
<h3>What we had to keep in mind</h3>
<ul>
<li>People choose an osteopath they trust. They want to understand what a session looks like and who will be treating them.</li>
<li>The Russian-speaking audience in one city is small, so ads wear out quickly.</li>
<li>We used posts from her profile as ads, so new people see her real content straight away.</li>
</ul>
<div class="q"><img class="av" src="/uploads/cases/tatyana-richmann-avatar.jpg" alt="" aria-hidden="true"><div><blockquote>In osteopathy, people go to a specialist they trust. So instead of discounts, the ads showed the work itself and clear explanations.</blockquote><cite><b>Tatyana Richmann</b>, Meta Ads specialist</cite></div></div>
<h2>Campaign setup</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Conversations.</b> The button opens Instagram Direct or WhatsApp, and the person writes to the osteopath directly.</li>
<li><b>Profile visits.</b> From September, low-cost Reels placements so people could get to know her and follow.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Audience.</b> Women aged 25+, the city and nearby areas.</p>

<h2>Ad creatives</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>A profile video launched in August.</b> The most conversations: 53 at ₪12.61. It was also saved most often (47 times) and brought 62 followers.</li>
<li><b>A photo ad, “An osteopath appointment”, with before and after photos of a back.</b> The cheapest conversations: 28 at ₪11.70.</li>
<li><b>A video from a session where she works with a patient.</b> 21 conversations at ₪15.54; 881 people watched it to the end.</li>
<li><b>A Reel, “Ever fallen on your tailbone?”</b> for profile visits: 885 visits at ₪0.21 and 27 followers.</li>
</ul>
<p class="muted" style="font-size:15px">We don't show the original creatives: the client hasn't agreed to publish them.</p>
<h2>Launch and results</h2>
<p>The first week was the best: 22 conversations at ₪9.24. Then the audience got used to the creatives, and by mid-September a conversation cost ₪28.72. On 23 September I launched new creatives and removed the placements that performed worse. The cost came back to ₪10–11 per conversation.</p>

<h3>Before → After: new creatives on 23 September</h3>
<p>Three weeks before and three weeks after the refresh, with the same budget.</p>
<div class="ba">
<div class="box"><small>Before · 4–24 September</small><b>₪19.27</b><span>per conversation, 28 conversations</span></div>
<div class="arr" aria-hidden="true">→</div>
<div class="box after"><small>After · 25 September – 15 October</small><b>₪12.05</b><span>per conversation, 43 conversations</span></div>
</div>
<p>The cost per conversation fell by 37%, and there were 54% more conversations. In the last days of October the cost started rising again, so it's time for fresh creatives.</p>
<h3>What else the data showed</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Women aged 35–44 wrote the most</b> (40 conversations), while women 65+ were the cheapest: ₪9.33 per conversation.</li>
<li><b>Facebook Feed conversations cost half as much as Instagram Feed ones</b>: ₪8.67 vs ₪17.70.</li>
<li><b>141 new profile followers</b>, and 111 of them came from the conversations ads.</li>
</ul>
