<h2>About the project</h2>
<p>A private dance teacher in Israel runs classes in several styles: Zumba for adults, High Heels and Jazz-Funk for young women, and hip-hop and modern dance groups for kids. Before me, the owner boosted posts herself. I have run her ads since 8 April 2026: in spring, then after a summer break from late August to 12 October.</p>
<h3>What we had to keep in mind</h3>
<ul>
<li>Each style has its own audience: Zumba is for adult women, High Heels for young women, kids' groups for parents.</li>
<li>The city is small, so the same creatives wear out quickly.</li>
<li>A newcomer's first step is a trial class, so that's what we had to sell.</li>
</ul>
<div class="q"><img class="av" src="/uploads/cases/tatyana-richmann-avatar.jpg" alt="" aria-hidden="true"><div><blockquote>In spring we had one shared ad for all styles. In autumn I split it: Zumba, High Heels and the kids' groups each got their own audience, copy and offer.</blockquote><cite><b>Tatyana Richmann</b>, Meta Ads specialist</cite></div></div>
<h2>What changed in autumn</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>A separate ad set for each style</b> instead of one shared audience.</li>
<li><b>A separate offer for each style:</b> a discounted trial class for adults and a free first class for kids.</li>
<li><b>One-tap sign-up</b>: the button opens WhatsApp directly.</li>
<li><b>A broader audience for Zumba.</b> The ads also reached women 45+, who are exactly the target audience for dance fitness.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Ad creatives</h2>
<p>For Zumba we ran one text with three visuals: two static images and a video. Both static images beat the video.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>A static Zumba ad</b> with the trial class right in the headline and the schedule and prices in the text. The cheapest conversations in the account: 43 at ₪5.92, CTR 9.58%.</li>
<li><b>A second static Zumba ad:</b> 47 conversations at ₪7.38. The video with the same text: 47 at ₪9.75.</li>
<li><b>A High Heels video with the teacher.</b> The best creative for this style: 48 conversations at ₪12.33, 29 follows, 16 saves.</li>
<li><b>A static ad for the kids' groups</b> with age groups and a free first class: 23 conversations at ₪12.38. The kids' video cost ₪27.81 per conversation.</li>
</ul>
<p class="muted" style="font-size:15px">We don't show the original creatives: the client hasn't agreed to publish them.</p>

<h3>Before → After: spring vs autumn</h3>
<p>We compare the cost per conversation in spring, when there was one shared ad, and in September, after the styles were split. The autumn budget was bigger, so we compare the cost, not the volume.</p>
<div class="ba">
<div class="box"><small>Spring · 8 April – 11 May</small><b>₪15.69</b><span>per conversation, 75 conversations</span></div>
<div class="arr" aria-hidden="true">→</div>
<div class="box after"><small>September</small><b>₪10.03</b><span>per conversation, 221 conversations</span></div>
</div>
<p>The cost per conversation fell by 36%, and people clicked the ads about 2.5 times as often.</p>

<h3>What else the data showed</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Women 45+ wrote a third cheaper</b> than women 25–44: ₪9.62 vs ₪14.79.</li>
<li><b>Facebook Feed brought almost half of all conversations</b>, at ₪9.83. In the Instagram Feed a conversation cost twice as much, ₪19.34.</li>
</ul>
